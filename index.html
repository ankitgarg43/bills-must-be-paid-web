🐷 Bills Must Be Paid

You're broke. The bills keep coming. There's only one way out: smash piggy banks.

1. Click / tap piggies to smash them for coins. Every swing tires your hand (stamina).
2. Pay each bill before its timer runs out — paying earns a free perk + Legacy Points.
3. Spend coins on the skill tree and better hammers. Watch for runner, lazy, armoured, golden and black-money piggies.
4. Bankruptcy isn't the end — it's prestige. Legacy rings & bracelets make every next run stronger.
5. Pay 15 bills to win and become Debt-Free.
